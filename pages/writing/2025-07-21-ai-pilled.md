# Get AI-pilled, fast
>  Release your IT-pilled brain from its rigid confines

{{date}}

If you're a developer of a certain age, then you've been indoctrinated into the IT school of thought. It has been drilled into your brain so hard that you reflexively model every problem in terms of relational databases and form submissions.

As soon as you hear someone start to describe a problem, you're already visualizing the Postgres schema in your head, and how to lay-out the UI. You even judge others by their ability to massage real-world problems into imperative programs.

You thought [this](https://xkcd.com/1425/) was a funny joke once:

![Always has been](assets/images/ai-brain-xkcd.png)

Haha, those ignorant wordcels! They've never written a line of SQL in their life, coming in hot with their [ignorant, impossible requirements](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/).

**Impossible became trivial in a hurry.**
**Ignorance became an advantage.**

And by the way, AI isn't just for identifying birds. [This is the best](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ts5lPDV--cU) way to ask a computer if a photo was taken in a national park.

**IT-pilled vs. AI-pilled**

My advice to you, IT-pilled developers of a certain age—is to get AI-pilled— **fast**.

The IT-pilled developer lets you have folders and tags.
The AI-pilled developer organizes your content for you.

The IT-pilled developer creates a rules engine with its own DSL and runtime.
The AI-pilled developer asks you what the rules should be.

The IT-pilled developer builds an importer that handles 40 known edge cases.
The AI-pilled developer handles every possible edge case.

The IT-pilled developer authors perfect email templates.
The AI-pilled developer authors perfect emails.

The IT-pilled developer learns regular expressions.
The AI-pilled developer does not.

Developers of a certain age—we've built our careers forcing people to think like a database, but now we have to start thinking like people.
